Project Overview -
App Insights Unlocked is a Power BI–based analytical case study using Google Play Store app data.
In this project, Power BI was used to clean, transform, analyze data, and answer structured business questions provided in the case study.

Note:- This project focuses on analytical problem-solving using Power BI.
A full interactive dashboard was not created; instead, all basic, medium, and advanced questions were solved using Power BI visuals, filters, and measures.

Business Objective - 
The objective of this analysis is to understand:
What factors contribute to app success on the Google Play Store ,
Which categories and genres perform best ,
How ratings, installs, size, price, and reviews are related ,
What insights can guide app development and marketing strategies ,

Dataset Information -
Source: Google Play Store Apps Dataset (Kaggle)

Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/lava18/google-play-store-apps

Data Cleaning & Preparation -
Performed directly inside Power BI:
Removed duplicate records ,
Handled missing ratings and size values ,
Converted Installs and Price to numeric format ,
Standardized category and genre names ,

Analysis Approach -
Instead of building a dashboard, Power BI was used to:
Answer Basic-level questions
(ratings, categories, free vs paid apps, content ratings) ,
Answer Medium-level questions
(correlation analysis, category performance, pricing impact) ,
Answer Advanced-level questions
(rating vs installs trend, update patterns, genre performance) ,

Power BI visuals such as tables, bar charts, column charts, scatter plots, and filters were used to support each answer.

Key Insights -
Free apps receive significantly higher installs and reviews ,
High installs do not always guarantee high ratings ,
Certain categories consistently show better user satisfaction ,
App size has limited impact on installs ,
Most apps target the “Everyone” content rating ,

Business Recommendations -
Focus on categories with high ratings and installs ,
Optimize app size for better adoption ,
Use free apps as an entry strategy for user acquisition ,
Maintain regular updates to sustain engagement
